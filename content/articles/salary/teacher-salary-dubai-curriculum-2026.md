Dubai has one of the most curriculum-diverse private school markets in the world. British, IB, American, Indian, and other curricula all compete for the same pool of international teachers. Curriculum type is one of the biggest factors in what you are actually offered.

## Why Curriculum Type Affects Pay

- School fee tiers often correlate with curriculum type, and higher-fee schools generally have larger budgets for teacher compensation.
- Qualification requirements differ. IB schools often require specific IB training or certification on top of a teaching qualification, which can affect the talent pool and pay expectations.
- Demand and supply differ by curriculum. IB-trained teachers are a smaller global talent pool relative to demand, which can support stronger compensation.

## What To Compare Beyond Base Salary

1. Housing allowance or provided accommodation, which is a major component of total package value for teachers relocating to Dubai.
2. Flight allowance, which is typically annual and may include dependents.
3. Tuition discount for your own children, which can represent major savings given Dubai private school fees.
4. End-of-service gratuity, which is calculated by UAE labor law based on years of service and final salary.
5. School KHDA rating, since higher-rated schools often have better-resourced compensation packages.

## Key Takeaways

- Curriculum type materially affects pay because it changes required qualifications and the school's fee and budget tier.
- IB-certified teachers are often in a smaller talent pool relative to demand, which can support stronger compensation.
- Do not compare base salary alone because housing, flights, and tuition discounts can shift total package value.
- A school's KHDA rating is a useful signal when comparing offers.

## Action Checklist

- [ ] Compare the full compensation package, not just base salary
- [ ] Ask whether housing is included in base pay or offered separately
- [ ] Review flight allowance, gratuity, and tuition benefits
- [ ] Compare school KHDA ratings and fee tiers
- [ ] Confirm current qualification requirements before applying
