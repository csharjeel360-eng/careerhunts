## What Is G42 and Why Its Careers Matter in 2026

G42 is an Abu Dhabi–based technology group focused on applied artificial intelligence, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, healthcare technology, and space systems. By 2026, G42 and its subsidiaries are among the region’s most active employers for specialized technical talent. "G42 careers" reflects a broad ecosystem of companies rather than a single employer, so identifying the correct subsidiary is a key step before applying.

## How to Apply (Concise Steps)

1. Visit the official portal: https://careers.g42.ai/global/en — this is the authoritative source for live vacancies.  
2. Filter by location, function, and company to find relevant openings.  
3. Read the posting carefully for required skills, reporting line, and any country-specific requirements.  
4. Tailor your CV and cover letter to match the role's technical keywords and outcomes.  
5. Apply through the portal; create and maintain your candidate account to track applications.  
6. Follow up politely if the job posting indicates an internal recruiter contact or application timeframe.

G42 hires across a broad set of technical and corporate functions, including:

- Cloud & Infrastructure (cloud engineers, DevOps, SRE)  
- Cybersecurity (SOC analysts, security engineers)  
- Data & Analytics (data engineers, analytics engineers)  
- Healthcare Tech (digital health engineers, clinical informaticists)  
- Space & Geospatial (satellite systems, remote sensing)  
- Corporate Functions (product, strategy, HR, commercial roles)

## Salary Expectations (Guidance)

Salary ranges vary widely by function, location, and level. Publicly reported ranges for technical roles often fall between approximately $60,000 and $130,000+ per year (USD, tax-free), with senior or research leadership roles potentially above this band. Treat these figures as planning estimates rather than guarantees.

## Benefits Snapshot

Typical benefits referenced in public materials and job postings include: competitive tax-free pay, bonus potential, comprehensive health insurance, learning and development programs, relocation support for international hires, and hybrid work policies depending on the role.

## Common Application Mistakes

- Applying to the wrong subsidiary without checking the posting.  
- Submitting a generic CV instead of aligning it to the role’s technical requirements.  
- Relying only on aggregator sites — always verify against the official portal.  
- Ignoring the job description’s required certifications or frameworks.

## Who Should Apply

Good fit: candidates with hands-on experience in AI/ML, cloud engineering, cybersecurity, data engineering, or healthcare technology who are open to hybrid or on-site work in the UAE.  
Less suitable: candidates seeking fully remote roles or purely entry-level positions with no demonstrated technical experience.

## Looking Ahead

G42’s hiring is likely to grow in specialized technical domains (AI, cloud, cybersecurity, healthtech, space). Monitor the central careers portal and follow relevant G42 subsidiaries on LinkedIn for up-to-date postings.
