Which licensing body you are certified under — DHA, HAAD or DOH, or MOH — determines not just where you can legally work as a nurse in the UAE, but often the pay band you are eligible for.

## The Three Licensing Bodies, Explained

- DHA: Dubai-based licensing for private hospitals, DHA-regulated clinics, and some free zone healthcare facilities.
- HAAD or DOH: Abu Dhabi-based licensing for government hospitals, SEHA facilities, and private Abu Dhabi healthcare providers.
- MOH: Used for work in the Northern Emirates, including Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain.

## Why Pay Differs Across Licenses

Nursing pay in the UAE is not purely about the license itself. It is also about where that license lets you work, and the healthcare markets in each emirate differ:

- Dubai has a large concentration of private hospitals and specialty clinics, often with more competitive packages for specialized nursing roles.
- Abu Dhabi has a strong government-linked hospital network, which can offer different benefit structures than private-sector-heavy Dubai.
- The Northern Emirates generally have a smaller, more cost-conscious private healthcare market, which can mean different base pay structures.

## What Affects Pay Beyond the License Itself

1. Specialization, because ICU, OR, oncology, and other specialized nursing roles typically command higher pay than general ward nursing.
2. Employer type, because government-linked facilities often have different benefit structures than private hospitals.
3. Housing and accommodation support, because some employers provide accommodation directly rather than a cash housing allowance.
4. License conversion costs, because moving between emirates can involve additional licensing and renewal costs.

## Key Takeaways

- Your license type determines where you can legally work, which indirectly shapes your pay options.
- Dubai's private hospital market and Abu Dhabi's government-linked facilities offer different compensation structures, not just different amounts.
- Specialization often matters more than license type alone.
- Always compare full packages, including housing, insurance, and allowances, rather than base salary alone.
