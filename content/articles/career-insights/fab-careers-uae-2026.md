If you're searching for a stable, well-paying job in the UAE banking sector, First Abu Dhabi Bank — known simply as FAB — is one of the strongest names to have on your radar in 2026. It's the largest bank in the UAE by assets, it's growing its digital and technology teams fast, and it runs one of the most structured hiring processes of any employer in the country. This guide breaks down exactly what FAB does, which roles are open right now, how much you can expect to earn, and the exact steps to apply — so you're not guessing your way through the process.

![FAB Careers UAE 2026 website feature section highlighting career growth, job stability, benefits, and opportunities at First Abu Dhabi Bank](/fab-careers-uae-2026-website-feature-section.webp)

## What Is First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB)?

FAB was formed in 2017 through the merger of the National Bank of Abu Dhabi and First Gulf Bank, and it has since grown into the UAE's largest bank and one of the biggest financial institutions in the Middle East. Headquartered in Abu Dhabi, the bank operates across more than 19 countries, serving personal, corporate, investment, wealth, and institutional clients both inside and outside the UAE.

![First Abu Dhabi Bank corporate image showing the bank's brand and scale in the UAE financial sector](/First%20Abu%20Dhabi%20Bank.webp)

With more than 7,500 employees and a network of over 100 branches, FAB isn't a small operation — it's a full-scale financial institution with divisions spanning retail banking, corporate and investment banking, wealth management, technology, risk, and compliance. That scale matters if you're job hunting, because it means FAB is almost always hiring for something, whether you're a fresh graduate or a senior banking professional.

## Why Consider a Career at FAB in 2026

A few things make FAB stand out compared to other UAE employers right now:

- **Stability.** As the UAE's largest bank, FAB offers the kind of job security that smaller fintechs and startups often can't match.
- **Career structure.** FAB runs formal graduate and leadership programs, so entry-level hires have a clear path upward rather than being left to figure things out.
- **Emiratisation focus.** FAB has invested heavily in structured programs for UAE Nationals, including the Ethraa Programme for fresh graduates and the Future Leaders Programme for experienced professionals looking to accelerate into leadership roles. If you're an expat wondering how this affects your own applications, see our guide on the [Emiratisation Quota 2026 for expat job seekers](https://careerhunt.online/career-resources/emiratisation-quota-2026-expat-jobseekers).
- **Digital transformation hiring.** Like most major Gulf banks in 2026, FAB is actively expanding its technology, data, and cybersecurity teams as it competes in digital banking — similar to the growth seen in other major UAE employers and technology-driven financial institutions.

## Popular Job Categories at FAB

![Banking career pathway infographic showing the progression from customer service, branch roles, specialist banking, to senior leadership in FAB careers](/Banking_Career_Pathway_Infographic.webp)

FAB hires across a wide range of functions. Here's how the roles generally break down:

### Customer Service, Contact Centre & Branch Banking

This is usually the largest hiring category and the easiest entry point for candidates with less experience. Common titles include customer service officer, personal banker, teller, sales officer, contact centre executive, and branch manager. FAB typically prioritizes strong communication skills, product knowledge, and attention to accuracy for these roles.

### Retail, Corporate & Investment Banking

For candidates with banking experience, FAB regularly hires relationship managers, credit analysts, trade finance specialists, treasury associates, and investment banking analysts. Senior openings extend up to assistant vice president, vice president, director, and managing director level.

### Digital, Data & Technology

This is where FAB's hiring has grown the most in recent years. Roles include software engineers, cloud specialists, cybersecurity analysts, data analysts, data scientists, and digital product managers. If you have hands-on experience with Python, SQL, AWS, Azure, or machine learning, this is one of the more competitive but rewarding paths into the bank.

### Risk, Compliance & Finance

FAB also hires steadily for risk management, KYC and onboarding, compliance officers, internal audit, and finance roles — functions that are core to any regulated financial institution operating at this scale.

### Islamic Banking, Legal & HR

Smaller in volume but consistently open, these functions support FAB's broader operations across its regional and international network.

## Benefits and Perks at FAB

Beyond base salary, most full-time roles at FAB come with:

- Medical and health insurance coverage
- Annual paid leave and flight allowances for eligible roles
- End-of-service gratuity, as per UAE labour law
- Structured training and professional development programs
- Housing or transport allowances for certain grades
- Performance-based bonuses tied to individual and bank results

Branch and customer-facing roles typically require on-site attendance, while some corporate, risk, and technology positions offer more flexible or hybrid arrangements.

## How to Apply for a Job at FAB (Step by Step)

1. **Go to the official FAB careers portal** at [bankfab.com/careers](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/about-fab/careers) or the dedicated jobs site at [careers.bankfab.com](https://careers.bankfab.com/).
2. **Filter open roles** by location, department, and experience level.
3. **Read the job description carefully**, including responsibilities, required qualifications, and whether the role is designated under FAB's Emiratisation initiatives.
4. **Prepare a tailored CV.** Highlight relevant banking experience, certifications, and measurable achievements rather than generic duties.
5. **Submit your application online**, including your resume and any required documents.
6. **Watch for talent community updates and walk-in interviews.** FAB periodically runs walk-in interview days for specific roles.

FAB also lists some positions on major job boards, but applying directly through the official portal is usually the safest and most direct route. ## FAB's Emiratisation Programs

If you're a UAE National, FAB runs two standout initiatives worth knowing about:

- **The Ethraa Programme** — designed for high-potential Emirati fresh graduates, sponsored by the Central Bank of the UAE and the Emirates Institute of Finance. It runs 8 to 12 months and combines structured learning, international qualifications, and hands-on project work with mentoring.
- **The Future Leaders Programme** — aimed at experienced UAE National professionals who want to accelerate into leadership roles within the bank.

Both programs are designed to build long-term banking careers rather than just fill vacancies, which is worth factoring in if you're early in your career and weighing your options.

## Tips to Stand Out When Applying to FAB

- **Be specific in your CV.** Instead of "handled customer queries," write "resolved an average of X customer queries daily with a Y% satisfaction rate." Numbers stand out in banking applications.
- **Match your CV to the role.** FAB spans retail banking, investment banking, and technology — a CV written for a customer service role won't work for a data analyst position, and vice versa.
- **Highlight relevant certifications.** For specialist and technology roles, credentials like CFA, FRM, CISSP, or CISM can meaningfully improve your chances.
- **Apply directly through the official portal.** This avoids third-party delays and ensures your application reaches FAB's actual hiring pipeline.
- **Follow up appropriately.** If you haven't heard back within a few weeks, a brief, polite follow-up email referencing the job ID is reasonable — but avoid repeated messages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is FAB a good company to work for in the UAE?**  
Yes — as the UAE's largest bank, FAB offers strong job stability, structured career development, and competitive benefits, making it one of the more sought-after employers in the region's financial sector.

**Does FAB hire fresh graduates?**  
Yes. FAB runs dedicated graduate programs, particularly for UAE Nationals through the Ethraa Programme, alongside general entry-level openings in customer service and branch banking.

**What qualifications do I need to work at FAB?**  
It depends on the role. Customer-facing and branch roles typically require a bachelor's degree and strong communication skills, while specialist roles in risk, technology, or investment banking often require relevant certifications and prior industry experience.

**Where can I find current FAB job openings?**  
The most up-to-date listings are on FAB's official careers portal at [bankfab.com/careers](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/about-fab/careers).
