ADNOC careers are managed through one official portal — **jobs.adnoc.ae** — covering roles across more than ten group companies, from ADNOC Onshore and Offshore to ADNOC Distribution, Drilling, and Logistics & Services. Openings run from entry-level technician and helper roles to senior engineering, finance, and project management positions, with pay that varies by entity, seniority, and whether the role is onshore, offshore, or corporate.

If you have searched for **ADNOC careers**, **ADNOC vacancies**, or **ADNOC salary**, this guide pulls the key information into one place: what ADNOC actually is, which companies under its umbrella hire directly, how the application process works, what salaries look like at different levels, and the separate Technical Academy pathway that many job seekers confuse with standard recruitment.

## Who Is ADNOC, and Why Does It Matter for Job Seekers?

The Abu Dhabi National Oil Company was established in 1971 to operate across the oil and gas industry and has grown into one of the largest energy producers in the world. For job seekers, the important thing is not just its size but its structure. ADNOC is not one employer with one HR department. It is a group of separately branded companies, each with its own hiring needs and career page, even though many applications flow through the same central portal.

## ADNOC Group Companies That Hire Separately

The main ADNOC entities that recruit include the following:

| Entity | What it does | Typical hiring focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ADNOC Onshore | Onshore oil and gas field operations | Petroleum engineers, operators, HSE staff |
| ADNOC Offshore | Offshore field production in the Arabian Gulf | Offshore technicians, marine crew, drilling support |
| ADNOC Drilling | Drilling operations and rig services | Drillers, rig engineers, maintenance technicians |
| ADNOC Refining | Refining and petrochemical feedstock operations | Process engineers, refinery operators |
| ADNOC Gas / Gas Processing | Natural gas processing and liquefaction | Gas plant operators, process technicians |
| ADNOC Distribution | Fuel and convenience retail in the UAE | Retail staff, station managers, supply chain |
| ADNOC Logistics & Services | Maritime and integrated logistics | Marine officers, logistics coordinators, shipping roles |
| Borouge | Petrochemicals joint venture | Chemical and process engineers, plant operators |
| TA'ZIZ | Industrial and chemical production venture | Project engineers, construction management |

A structural point worth knowing is that ADNOC has reorganized some of its listed affiliates over time, which is why you may sometimes see references to XRG alongside entities such as ADNOC Distribution or ADNOC Gas in ownership disclosures. Day-to-day hiring and career pages are usually unaffected.

## What Roles Are Currently Open

Active roles typically span finance, project management, drilling operations, energy distribution, logistics, and technical maintenance, with a heavy concentration around Abu Dhabi and offshore field assignments. Common categories include:

- Engineering and technical roles, including process, mechanical, instrumentation, and electrical positions
- Operations and maintenance roles, such as plant operators, technicians, and offshore crew
- Finance and commercial roles, including analysts, traders, and procurement specialists
- Project management roles for large capital projects and strategic programs
- Entry-level and support roles, especially in the wider ADNOC ecosystem and its service partners
- Corporate and HQ functions, including HR, legal, communications, and digital roles

## ADNOC Salary in the UAE: Realistic Ranges

ADNOC does not publish a public salary scale, so any figure you see online should be treated as an estimate. Typical ranges are roughly:

- Technician or entry-level operator: around AED 10,000–15,000 per month
- Mid-level engineer or specialist: around AED 18,000–30,000 per month
- Senior engineer or team lead: around AED 30,000–45,000 per month
- Senior project manager or senior specialist: AED 50,000+ per month

Offshore and drilling roles often carry a premium because of rotations, site conditions, and field requirements.

## How to Apply for ADNOC Jobs: Step by Step

1. Go to the official portal at **jobs.adnoc.ae**.
2. Create a candidate profile and upload an updated CV tailored to the entity and role you are targeting.
3. Search and filter by entity, location, and job category rather than browsing everything at once.
4. Submit your application and wait for screening and eligibility checks.
5. Prepare for technical assessments or interviews depending on the role.
6. If successful, review your offer letter carefully before accepting.
7. Complete onboarding, medical checks, and any visa processing steps if required.

A genuine warning is worth repeating: legitimate ADNOC recruitment does not charge fees at any stage of the process. If anyone asks for payment for an offer, visa, or training, it is a scam.

## ADNOC Technical Academy: The Pathway for UAE Nationals

A large share of ADNOC-related search traffic is actually looking for the **ADNOC Technical Academy (ATA)**. This pathway is specifically for **UAE Nationals**, not international applicants. The academy trains Emirati talent for frontline technical roles and is widely associated with the oil and gas operations stream.

Alongside ATA, ADNOC also runs the **Accelerator Program**, which includes tracks for fresh graduates, scholars, internships, and early-career Emirati students. If you are an expatriate professional, your route is the standard vacancy process through the portal rather than these early-career programs.

## FAQs

**Is ADNOC hiring in 2026?**
Yes. ADNOC and its group companies post vacancies year-round, though volumes shift with capital project cycles.

**How do I check ADNOC job vacancies?**
Visit jobs.adnoc.ae directly and filter by entity and category.

**What is ADNOC's average salary?**
There is no single average because it depends on entity, role, and seniority. Entry-level technicians tend to sit around AED 10,000–15,000 per month, while senior specialists can exceed AED 50,000.

**Does ADNOC charge recruitment fees?**
No. Legitimate ADNOC recruitment never asks candidates to pay for offers, visas, or training.

**Can non-UAE nationals join ADNOC Technical Academy?**
No. ATA and the Accelerator Program are specifically for UAE Nationals.

## Key Takeaways

- ADNOC recruitment runs through one official portal, jobs.adnoc.ae, covering all group companies.
- The ADNOC umbrella includes multiple entities, so it is better to search and apply by entity rather than only by the ADNOC brand name.
- Salary estimates range roughly from AED 10,000 per month for entry-level technician roles to AED 50,000+ for senior specialists and project managers.
- ADNOC Technical Academy and the Accelerator Program are for UAE Nationals only; expatriates apply through standard vacancies.
- ADNOC never charges recruitment fees.
