Sharjah Aviation Services (SAS) is the ground-handling and cargo operator at Sharjah International Airport, and it hires regularly across ramp operations, cargo handling, passenger services, and administration. If you're looking for Sharjah Aviation Services careers in 2026, this guide explains the most common roles, expected salaries, hiring requirements, and the best ways to apply for jobs at one of the UAE's most active aviation support employers.

## What Is Sharjah Aviation Services?

Sharjah Aviation Services is the ground services provider contracted to handle flights operating out of Sharjah International Airport (SHJ) — one of the busiest cargo hubs in the UAE. Where the airport authority itself manages the facility, SAS handles the day-to-day operational work: loading and unloading aircraft, managing baggage, coordinating ramp activity, and supporting passenger check-in and boarding for the airlines that fly through Sharjah.

For job seekers searching for Sharjah Aviation Services careers, it helps to understand that SAS is a separate employer from the airport authority and often hires through different channels, including walk-in interviews and operational contractor recruitment.

This distinction matters for job seekers. Many candidates search only for "Sharjah Airport jobs" and miss SAS entirely, even though it's one of the largest employers physically based at the airport.

| Role | Department | Typical Requirement |

| Cargo Handler | Cargo Services | Basic English, forklift certification a plus |

Ramp and baggage-handling roles tend to have the highest hiring volume since they turn over more frequently and don't always require prior aviation experience — making them the easiest entry point for freshers.

## Salary Expectations for Sharjah Aviation Services Jobs

Exact figures vary by role, contract type, and experience, but general ranges reported across UAE ground-handling roles:

- Baggage Handler / Ramp Agent: AED 2,000–3,000/month
- Cargo Handler: AED 2,500–3,500/month
- Passenger Service Agent: AED 3,000–4,500/month
- Supervisor roles: AED 5,000–8,000/month, depending on experience

Most entry-level ground positions include accommodation, transport, and medical insurance as part of the package — worth confirming directly in the offer letter rather than assuming it's included.

## How to Apply for Sharjah Aviation Services Careers

To improve your chances of landing Sharjah Aviation Services jobs, prepare your documents carefully and focus on the practical requirements employers usually look for in ground-handling and airport support roles.

1. Prepare your documents — updated CV, passport copy, and any relevant certifications (forklift license, airside driving permit, first aid, etc.)
2. Check for walk-in interview announcements — ground-handling companies in the UAE frequently run walk-in hiring days for high-turnover roles like ramp and baggage handling
3. Apply through official channels — avoid third-party agents requesting upfront fees; legitimate UAE employers do not charge candidates to apply
4. Highlight physical fitness and shift flexibility — ground roles are physically demanding and operate around flight schedules, including nights and holidays
5. Follow up professionally — a short, polite follow-up after one to two weeks is standard practice in UAE hiring
