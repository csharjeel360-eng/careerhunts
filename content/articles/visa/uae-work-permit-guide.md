A UAE work permit is the labor authorization that lets an employer legally hire a foreign national for a specific role. It is usually issued first, and the residence visa or entry permit follows as part of the broader employment process.

## What the Work Permit Covers

- Employer-side approval and labor authorization
- Role-specific eligibility and hiring permissions
- Connection to the residence visa process for legal stay
- Documentation that confirms the job and company sponsorship

## Why the Permit Matters

The permit is important because it proves the employer has legal permission to hire you under UAE labor rules. Without the correct approvals, your employment status and residence authorization can be affected.

## Common Questions

1. Confirm who applies for the permit and whether the employer is authorized to do so.
2. Review the role, salary, and sponsor details before signing any employment contract.
3. Keep copies of all approvals and visa-related documents for your records.
