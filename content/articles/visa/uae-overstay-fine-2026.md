As of 11 February 2026, the UAE unified its overstay fine at a flat AED 50 per day across all seven emirates and all visa types, including tourist, visit, residence, and cancelled residence permits. The old 10-day grace period for tourist and visit visas has also been removed, so the fine clock starts the day after your visa expires with no buffer.

## How the New Fine Actually Works

- Flat rate: AED 50 per day, regardless of which emirate you are in or which visa category you hold.
- No grace period for tourist and visit visas: the cushion that used to apply before fines were charged is gone.
- Cancelled residence visas are different: these still carry a separate grace period of 30 to 180 days depending on your skill classification before the daily fine kicks in.
- Overstays beyond 30 days usually require an exit permit before you can leave the country.

This table is a simple multiplication example for illustration. Always confirm your exact fine and any extra exit-permit fees through the official GDRFA or ICP fine-checking service before paying or traveling, because additional charges can apply depending on your case.

## Why the Grace Period Removal Matters

The old system gave travelers a small buffer for things like missed flights, sudden illness, or administrative delays. That buffer no longer exists for tourist and visit visas. Practically, this means:

- Book your exit flight with margin and avoid cutting it close to your visa expiry date.
- If you get sick or face an emergency near your visa expiry, contact GDRFA or ICP immediately rather than assuming a grace period will cover you.
- Residency renewal and cancellation processing is now linked to traffic fine clearance, so unpaid traffic fines can pause your visa renewal and should be cleared early.

## What To Do If You Have Already Overstayed

1. Check your exact fine through the official GDRFA Dubai or ICP portal rather than estimating and paying blind.
2. Settle any outstanding traffic fines first if you are renewing residency, because this can now block processing.
3. If you are beyond 30 days, arrange your exit permit before attempting to leave the country.
4. If your residence visa was cancelled, check your specific grace period before assuming the daily fine applies immediately.

## Key Takeaways
